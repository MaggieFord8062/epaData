Method and limitations

How it's calculated

For each record, each pollutant's mass is converted from short tons to kilograms (1 short ton = {{ short_ton_kg }} kg) and multiplied by its TRACI factor. A category's result is the sum over the pollutants that have a factor in that category. If a record is missing any pollutant a category needs, that record is left out of the category rather than counted as 0. Per MWh results divide the category total by the gross load of the same records.
