How to read these charts

Totals and ratios

Amounts that add up (emissions, gross load, heat input) are shown as totals. Ratios such as CO2 intensity and heat rate are shown as overall ratios, the total of one divided by the total of the other, so large units count more than small ones.

Colors

Each color always means the same thing on every chart: CO2, coal and group A are blue; SO2, gas and group B are orange; NOx and oil and other fuels are green. Maps use one blue from light (low) to dark (high).

Log scales

A few very large plants emit far more than most units, so distributions and scatter plots use a log scale by default, where each gridline is ten times the one before. Values of 0 can't be shown on a log scale and are counted in the chart notes.

Correlation

Correlation shows whether two measurements tend to move together. It does not show that one causes the other.
